Backhaul Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Backhaul (“the app”) is operated by Northgrey Enterprises Inc. (“we”, “us”). Backhaul connects shippers who post loads with carriers who make offers on them. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information: your name, email address, phone number, company name, and whether you use Backhaul as a shipper, a carrier, or both.

Load and offer information: for loads you post, the pickup and delivery addresses and business names, cargo details (description, weight, dimensions, piece count), pickup and delivery timing, loading and access notes, acceptable vehicle types, whether the load is hand bomb (loaded and unloaded by hand), and any photos of the load you add (up to four). For trailer moves, the trailer type, length, weight, whether it is loaded, the hitch and ball size, and whether its lights and brakes work. For livestock loads, the type of animal, number of head, approximate weight, and whether loading chutes are available. If you choose to repeat a load, the days of the week it should be posted again. For offers you make as a carrier, the price (flat, hourly, or per-kilometre), your pickup availability, your estimated delivery time, and any message you add.

Location information: the pickup and delivery addresses you enter, and the base location a carrier chooses to save. We convert these to map coordinates, estimate the route distance, and use a carrier’s base location to show nearby loads first and to send alerts for loads within the distance they choose.

Delivery proof: when a carrier records a pickup or delivery, we collect any photo, signature, recipient name, and note they add, along with the time it was recorded.

Messages: once a load is booked, the shipper and the selected carrier can message each other in the app. We store those messages, any photos sent in them, and when each message was delivered to and read by the other person.

Carrier credentials: if you are a carrier, the CVOR number, cargo insurance company, and insurance expiry date you choose to enter.

Invoice information: if you are a carrier and create invoices in Backhaul, your business mailing address, HST/GST number, and tax rate, and for each invoice its number, amount, tax, date, and whether you have marked it paid. Invoice PDFs and QuickBooks export files are created on your phone and shared only where you choose to send them.

Reports: if you report a user, load, offer, or review, we collect the reason, any details you add, and what you reported.

Ratings and comments: after a delivery, a shipper can rate the carrier (punctuality, price accuracy, and reliability) and leave comments.

Device and usage information: a push notification token, device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic or crash data.

Subscription information: Backhaul is currently free to use. If we offer a paid subscription in the future, it will be purchased and billed through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Google or Apple processes your payment, and we do not receive or store your payment card number. If you subscribe, we receive your subscription status, plan, purchase and renewal dates, and a transaction identifier.

Communications: messages you send us for support.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • To let shippers post loads and carriers receive notifications and make offers
  • To let shippers compare offers and select a carrier
  • To let the shipper and the booked carrier message each other
  • To record pickups and deliveries and show carrier ratings
  • To close loads automatically once their pickup date has passed without a booking
  • To post your repeating loads again on the days you choose
  • To let carriers create numbered invoices for delivered loads and track whether they have been paid
  • To review reports and act on accounts or content that break our Terms of Service
  • If you subscribe, to manage your subscription, verify that it is active, and handle billing questions
  • To send notifications about loads, offers, and account activity
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the app secure
  • To improve and troubleshoot the app

3. How information is shared

With other users: when you post a load, carriers can see its details, including any photos you add to it. Until you book a carrier, carriers see only the town for pickup and delivery; the exact addresses, business names and access notes are shared only with the carrier you book. When a carrier makes an offer, the shipper can see the offer and the carrier’s profile and ratings. A carrier’s CVOR number and insurance details are shown to shippers who receive that carrier’s offers. These are entered by the carrier and are not checked by Backhaul. Once a shipper selects an offer, the shipper and the selected carrier can see each other’s contact details, their messages and chat photos to each other (no one else can see chat photos), and the delivery proof so they can complete the shipment. The carrier also sees the shipper’s billing email (or, if none is set, the email address the shipper signs in with) so they can send an invoice. If the carrier emails the invoice from the app, we send it to that billing email on the carrier’s behalf through our email provider. The shipper sees the carrier’s invoice number, amount, and paid status for that load.

When you report something, the Backhaul team receives the report. The person you reported is not told who reported them.

Backhaul does not process freight payments. Payment for a shipment is arranged directly between the shipper and the carrier.

With service providers who help us run the app, such as hosting, database, and file storage (Supabase), push notifications (Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging), address search, maps, and route distances (Google Maps Platform, Esri, OpenStreetMap, Photon by Komoot, and the OSRM routing service, which receive the addresses, map areas, or pickup and drop-off points you look up), email delivery for invoices and account messages (Resend), and, if you subscribe, subscription management (Google Play, the Apple App Store and RevenueCat). They may only use your information to provide services to us.

Where your information is stored: our database and file storage are hosted by Supabase in the United States (Virginia). Some of our other service providers also process information in the United States or other countries. Information stored outside Canada is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessed by its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities. By using Backhaul, you understand that your information will be stored and processed outside Canada.

For legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention and account deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app at any time. When you do, we immediately remove your personal details (name, phone number, company name, email address or login, base location, business address, HST/GST number, and CVOR and insurance details), the messages and chat photos you sent, your profile photo, your notifications and device tokens, loads you posted that were never booked, and offers that were not accepted.

Records that another user also depends on, such as delivered loads, accepted offers, shipping documents, ratings, and delivery proof, are kept for that other user’s records and for dispute resolution. They are kept for 7 years, are no longer linked to your name or contact details, and appear under “Deleted user”. Your private rating comments are deleted. You cannot delete your account while you have booked or in-transit loads.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your profile information in the app.
  • You can turn off notifications in your device settings.
  • If you have a subscription, you can manage or cancel it at any time in the Google Play Store (Payments & subscriptions) or, on iPhone, in Settings under your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a subscription, so please cancel it first.
  • You can delete your account in the app or by following the instructions at https://backhaulapp.ca/delete-account.html.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your information. Contact us to make a request.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Backhaul is intended for business use by adults. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the date above. If changes are significant, we will notify you in the app or by email.
