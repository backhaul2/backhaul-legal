How it works

One simple flow for both sides of the load.

Shippers Post it. Pick a carrier.

  1. Post a loadPickup, drop-off, what it is and when it is ready. Freight, trailer moves and livestock are all covered.
  2. Compare offersCarriers send flat, hourly or per-kilometre offers. See their ratings, insurance and CVOR details before you choose.
  3. Book and follow alongMessage your carrier in the app and get alerts when it is booked and delivered, with proof of delivery on file.
  4. Receive one clean invoiceThe carrier invoices the agreed amount with HST or GST, straight to your billing email.

Carriers Find loads. Get paid.

  1. Find loads near youSet your base and your vehicle types. Nearby loads show first, with alerts when new ones are posted.
  2. Send your offerQuote the way you work: a flat price, an hourly rate or a rate per kilometre.
  3. Haul and recordRecord pickup and delivery, with a photo and the name of who received it as proof.
  4. Invoice from your own emailCreate a numbered invoice in a tap and send it from your own email address, with the PDF attached. Export to QuickBooks any time.
