Get Backhaul

Install it on your Android phone, or use it in your browser on an iPhone or computer. One account works everywhere.

Your phone

Android

The full app, with alerts when a load is posted near you.

Download for Android
  1. Tap Download for Android. If you opened this link in WhatsApp, tap ⋮ and choose Open in Chrome first.
  2. Samsung phones: go to Settings › Security and privacy › Auto Blocker and turn it off. You can turn it back on after installing.
  3. When the download finishes, tap Open.
  4. If your phone asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  5. Tap Install. If a warning says the app is from an unknown developer, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  6. Open Backhaul and create your account.

Updates arrive by themselves. The app will also be on Google Play soon.

Your phone

iPhone

The iPhone app is coming to the App Store. Until then, use Backhaul in Safari. It does everything the app does except phone alerts.

Open Backhaul
  1. Tap Open Backhaul and create your account.
  2. To keep it on your home screen, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
