Common questions

Quick answers. If yours is not here, email us.

How do I install Backhaul?

Go to backhaulapp.ca/download. On Android, tap Download for Android and follow the steps on that page. On a Samsung phone, first turn off Auto Blocker in Settings › Security and privacy; you can turn it back on afterwards. On an iPhone or computer, tap Open Backhaul to use it in your browser. The iPhone app is coming to the App Store.

I can’t sign in

Check that you are using the email address you signed up with, and follow the code or link Backhaul emails you. Look in your spam or junk folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes. Still stuck? Email us from that address and we will help.

Am I a shipper or a carrier? Can I change it?

Each account is either a shipper (posts loads) or a carrier (sends offers and hauls), chosen when you set up your profile. If you need both, use a separate email address for a second account. If you picked the wrong one by mistake, contact us.

How do I send an invoice?

Carriers: after a load is delivered, open it in the app and tap Create invoice. The amount is set by the offer you were booked on. Then tap Send from my email to open your own email app with the shipper’s billing email and the PDF attached. You can also tap Via Backhaul to have Backhaul email it for you, or Share PDF (Download PDF on a computer).

How do I get paid?

Backhaul does not process payments. Shippers pay carriers directly under the terms on the invoice, such as Net 30. Mark the invoice as paid in the app when the money arrives.

The shipper says they did not get my invoice

Ask them to check their spam folder and confirm the billing email on their profile. You can resend the invoice from the load at any time.

How do I report a user or a load?

Open the load, offer or profile in the app and use the report option. You can also block a user from your account. For anything urgent, email us.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account › Delete account. If you cannot open the app, email us from your account address. Full details are on the account deletion page.
